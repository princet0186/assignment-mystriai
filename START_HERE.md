# Mystri | Build something worth trusting

Choose **one** of the two challenges below. Spend **up to four hours in total**, including setup, research, testing and your handover. Stop at the limit and tell us what remains. A smaller, verified result can score better than an ambitious, unreliable one.

You have **seven calendar days from the assessment invitation** to submit. This is a scheduling window, not seven days of work: the total work limit remains four hours, which you may split across sessions.

| Choose | Your mission | Most relevant to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Track A: Repair the register** | Investigate and improve a small invoice application. | Audit product engineering |
| **Track B: Find the worthwhile automation** | Investigate a business and technical claim, then test your recommendation with a working prototype. | Technical research and prototyping |

Both roles involve building software. Your choice tells us your preference; it does not determine your eventual role. Please do not complete both. There is no extra credit for doing so or for exceeding four hours.

## AI is welcome

Use AI, search, documentation and libraries as you normally would. Any model or free tool is fine; no paid plan, API spend, cloud deployment or special hardware is required. We assess the decisions you make, the evidence you gather and the result you can explain. We do not score model choice, prompt length, commit count or time spent online.

Work individually. Credit copied code and external sources. In your handover, give two or three brief examples of how you used AI or another tool and what you checked, changed or rejected. Full chat histories are not required. If you did not use AI, say so and describe your verification process.

## Start here

1. Read the two brief PDFs or the `README.md` in each track, then pick one. Reading and choosing are included in the four hours.
2. Extract this ZIP before running anything. Track A needs Python 3.10 or newer and a browser. Track B includes an optional Python starter, but you may use another language.
3. Complete only the chosen track. Keep notes as you go; elaborate presentation is unnecessary.
4. Copy `SUBMISSION_TEMPLATE.md` into your chosen folder as `HANDOVER.md` and fill it in.

## Work independently

Use your best judgment within the stated requirements. Where details are unspecified, make reasonable assumptions, document them and proceed. Include important questions you would investigate in a real project in your handover. We will not provide solution guidance during the assessment.

Contact the inviter only for inaccessible or missing assessment materials, or an accessibility adjustment. For ordinary setup or coding problems, use documentation and tools, record the issue and continue with the work you can complete. The deliberately broken behaviour in Track A is part of the task. Stop at four hours and explain any remaining blocker.

## What to send

Reply to your assessment invitation within seven calendar days, with:

- An accessible download link to one ZIP named `YourName_TrackA.zip` or `YourName_TrackB.zip`, or an accessible repository link. Include your chosen track, changed code, tests, outputs and `HANDOVER.md`.
- Commands a reviewer can copy to run your work. Include a dependency file if you add dependencies.
- For Track B, also include your decision note and source list as described in its brief.

Exclude virtual environments, `node_modules`, caches, generated working databases, credentials and the unused track. Track A submissions should retain the supplied `fixtures/` files. Check the link's access permissions for the inviter; you do not need a public repository. Send a link rather than attaching source-code archives to email. No slide deck, recorded video or hosted demo is required. Keep the assessment private while recruitment is ongoing.

These are fictional exercises using synthetic data. Do not contact real customers, send messages from the prototype or use confidential material from past employers. Your submission is for recruitment evaluation, not a production deliverable for Mystri.

## How we evaluate

We look for clear problem selection, dependable execution, useful verification, thoughtful use of tools and an honest handover. Each track publishes its scoring weights. Reviewers may run additional cases that follow the published requirements, including changed input records. Record what you actually executed and observed; a claim of correctness alone is not verification.

Shortlisted applicants will have a **15-20 minute follow-up discussion** to explain a decision, predict the effect of a small change and adapt their own work. AI tools remain allowed. This discussion is separate from the four-hour take-home limit. Unfinished work is welcome when its limits are clear.
